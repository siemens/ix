Add `hide-divider` to **ix-key-value** and `hide-dividers` to **ix-key-value-list** to hide the divider lines. Dividers are shown by default.

**Breaking:** **ix-key-value** now shows its divider for every `label-position`, matching the design. Set `hide-divider` to keep standalone `label-position="left"` items without a divider. See `BREAKING_CHANGES/v6.md`.
